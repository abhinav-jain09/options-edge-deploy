The adversarial sweep produced these findings, most severe first.

1. **P1 — Strike archival still advances past unread committed records.**

   **Evidence:** End offsets are captured at [archive:318](/private/tmp/oe-deploy-fpstrike/scripts/ops/archive/oe-archive-kafka.sh:318), consumption is committed-only at [552](/private/tmp/oe-deploy-fpstrike/scripts/ops/archive/oe-archive-kafka.sh:552), any positive non-STRICT capture with consumer status zero passes at [577](/private/tmp/oe-deploy-fpstrike/scripts/ops/archive/oe-archive-kafka.sh:577), and the original end becomes the checkpoint at [620](/private/tmp/oe-deploy-fpstrike/scripts/ops/archive/oe-archive-kafka.sh:620).

   Executing the actual acceptance condition produced:

   ```text
   endoff=1200, got=1099, consumer_rc=0
   → ACCEPT checkpoint=1200 records=1099
   ```

   An unresolved transaction beginning at offset `1100` can therefore commit after capture, while the next run resumes at `1200`. ### Why the console-consumer topics keep their discovery

The same silence does apply to them. §16i asserts it: a console topic against the recorded unreachable broker is still `SKIP`, `absent=1 failed=0`, rc 0. But the change is not demonstrably safe for every existing topic, so it is not made here.

- **It would stop archiving data that is archived today.** A per-partition `Skip getting offsets` on a console topic now omits one partition, and the others are still captured. Most of the 189 prod topics expire in one day. Failing the whole topic, as the committed path does, would give up the readable partitions of a topic whose data is gone tomorrow. That trades data for accounting. For strike (retention -1, one partition) the trade does not arise.
- **The console path's describe failure is designed to fall back, not to fail.** Section 7 pins "older broker, or a kafka-topics.sh that fails: the offset detector must still work". Making an unreadable describe fatal for those topics reverses a tested behaviour on 189 topics.
- **The consequences differ.** A failed console read never advances a checkpoint, and no console checkpoint feeds a source-destruction interlock: the marker is strike-only. The P1/P2 harm, a wipe authorised on an unproven archive, cannot happen there. For those topics the 20:00 `oe-archive-verify.sh` cron already reports a date that nothing was archived for as MISSING or PARTIAL, and alerts.

An accounting-only change for console topics is possible later: count a non-zero, non-"Could not match" answer as `failed` while still archiving every readable partition. It is recorded as a follow-up, not made here.
